# Contributing

Issues are welcome. Pull requests from outside contributors aren't accepted at this time.

## What helps in an issue

- What you were doing, what you expected, and what happened.
- The version (shown in the header, the footer and the startup line).
- Your OS and Python version.
- For a decoding problem, a small PCAP that reproduces it, **with nothing confidential in it**. Don't
  attach a capture from a client's network. Trim it, synthesise it, or describe the frame structure.

## Forks

The licence lets you fork it and change it. Keep it under the same licence, say what you changed, and
publish your source if you distribute it or let others use it over a network.
